On Sunday, our group spent time in Bobst preparing or our presentation on monday we got to share our contributions with the class.<!--more--> I'm glad we got to present first, because I am proud of the work we completed. Our contributions include ones that improve functionality as well as ones that reduce work for future developers. Two days after our presnetation, another of our pull requests was merged! [Here it is](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/pull/47771)! This is an important enhancement because it implements a new feature (rendering custom theme descriptions), as well as fixing a previous issue (only being able to render one custom theme at a time).

The other groups had some good things to share. I was impressed by some of the changes others had made and it was visible who was invested in making real enhancements to their projects. Unfortunately, many people spent much of their presentation time introducing their projects: the codebase, different functionalities and features. What matters most though is learning how to contribute and sharing their work. I also noticed others explaining very small, granular details about the code changes in their contributions. And then they lost me. I want to know the impact of the changes, not necessarily how the changes were made.

But overall, the presentations have been interesting, and I enjoyed getting to share our work.
